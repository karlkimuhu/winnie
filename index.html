Season One

Four episodes so far, and you're in every one of them.

I The Notes

I texted you from the group chat, for notes. Officially.

I texted you from the church group chat — needed notes, nothing more (or so I told myself). Around that time, someone at church swore up and down you were his girlfriend. You denied it, loudly, more than once, and it turned out to be nonsense. If I'm honest, my own eyes weren't fully sorted out yet either. But somewhere between the notes and the denials, you became the only person I was actually texting.

II The Letters

Some things deserved more than a text.

There's something a text can't do that handwriting can. So I started writing to you — actual letters, folded up, meant only for you. I don't know if you kept any of them. I hope you did.

III The Storm

Not every episode is easy to watch back.

That road trip left something in me I still think about. Hearing that you still had unfinished business with your ex made me feel like I'd always come second — to him, to your friends, to anything that wasn't me. We still haven't fully talked it through, if I'm honest. But you've told me, over and over, that there's nothing there, and I choose to believe you — over him, over anyone, every single day. And somewhere in that storm, I became sure of exactly how much I feel for you.

Soundtrack: "Best Part" — Daniel Caesar ft. H.E.R.
IV The Yes

I half-expected you to say no.

I asked you to be my girlfriend in church, half-expecting a no — after everything, I didn't think I deserved a yes. You gave me one anyway. I'm still shocked by it some days. I think about that yes more often than you probably know. And however you're reading this right now, this is me giving you my heart — plainly, no better way to say it.
